Dewfall Lantern

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Dewfall Lantern is a cozy puzzle game. We built it to collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

  • No account, no personal data. The game requires no sign-up, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or photos.
  • Anonymous leaderboard scores. When you complete a daily puzzle, the app sends your solve time together with a randomly generated device identifier (a random UUID created on your device) to our leaderboard service (hosted on Supabase, EU region). This identifier is not linked to you and cannot identify you. It is used only to rank daily solve times.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Local data. Your progress (levels, streaks, coins, settings) is stored on your device only.

What we don't do

  • No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no analytics profiles.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.

Data retention & deletion

Leaderboard entries contain only a random identifier and solve times. To request deletion, contact us with your approximate play dates and we will remove the entries: guvenser09@gmail.com. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
