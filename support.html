Common questions

My progress disappeared

Progress (levels, streaks, coins, settings) is stored on your device only, so deleting the app or switching to a new device clears it. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID: tap Settings → Restore purchases to bring back anything you bought.

I bought coins but they didn't arrive

Open Settings → Restore purchases first. If the coins still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

How do I change my leaderboard name?

Open Settings → Display name. Your new name replaces the old one on the daily, monthly and all-time boards.

I saw an offensive display name

Press and hold the entry on the leaderboard to report or hide it, or email guvenser09@gmail.com. We review reports and remove offending names within 24 hours.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Data → Delete my data. This erases your leaderboard entries, display name and invite code from our server, clears your local progress and issues your device a new random identifier. It cannot be undone. You can also email us and we will remove your data for you.

A puzzle looks impossible

Every puzzle is generated with a guaranteed unique solution reachable by pure logic — no guessing is ever required. If you are stuck, a hint will point at a single safe move. If you believe a puzzle really is broken, email us the date and the puzzle type and we will check it.
